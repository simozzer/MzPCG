The first release of MzPCG: the PCF's idea on the volume, a pattern-controlled gain, as a VST3 effect.

- **32 patterns** - MzPCF's - struck on every step of your project, sample-accurately, through loops and tempo changes, at 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32.
- **GATE** for trance gates and chops, **DUCK** for sidechain-style pumping.
- **DEPTH**, **ATK**, **HOLD** and **DEC** shape every step, from a square gate to a pluck to a swell; it never clicks. **MIX** and **LEVEL** for use as an insert.
- A display that draws the gain the pattern makes with the knobs as they are, with the step playing lit and a meter of the gain now.
- With the transport stopped, the sound passes untouched.

Known limits:
- **Windows only** for now.
- **Changing MODE while playing jumps the gain** between gating and ducking.
